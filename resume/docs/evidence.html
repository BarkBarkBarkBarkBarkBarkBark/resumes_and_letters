Dhn Fidelity

Validated · BarkBarkBarkBarkBarkBarkBark/DHN_validation
Revision: 496c2326f000037698f7b92b4b3a4139c3a70d13

What was verified
Code and archived outputs cross-checked; raw source data unavailable for an independent rerun.
Measurement definition
Elementwise CSC-minus-MED difference in microvolts; maximum absolute difference and nonzero count, before and after numpy.round of CSC.
Test conditions
First 20,000,000 frames of one channel at 32,000 samples/s; MED contigua concatenated; physical-unit scaling.
Result
19,951,989 nonzero differences; maximum absolute error 0.5 uV. After whole-microvolt source rounding: zero mismatches. Compared duration is 625 s, derived as frames / sample rate.
Limitations & applicability
Not bit-exact relative to unrounded source. Mean error, compression ratio, file-size reduction, and encoder lossless/lossy settings are not established by these outputs. Do not generalize to complete sessions or other configurations.

Dhn Spike Control

Validated · BarkBarkBarkBarkBarkBarkBark/DHN_validation
Revision: 496c2326f000037698f7b92b4b3a4139c3a70d13

What was verified
Archived code/output agreement inspected; no raw-data rerun or new sorting performed.
Measurement definition
Exact intersection of the first sorted unit's spike-frame arrays; Jaccard = intersection / union with zero frame tolerance.
Test conditions
Same 20-million-frame single-channel slice; both inputs scaled to uV and rounded to int32; canonical arrays asserted equal. Bandpass 300-6000 Hz; local whitening seed 0; MountainSort5 scheme 2 with internal filter/whiten disabled.
Result
35,587 CSC spikes and 35,587 MED spikes; 35,587 exact frame matches; no source-only events; exact Jaccard 1.0.
Limitations & applicability
Controlled consistency of equal rounded inputs, not proof of unrounded fidelity, biological unit correctness, all-unit conservation, real-time performance, or algorithm authorship.
Exact sources
  • sort_med/evidence/Strict_Comparison_of_Sorted_Spikes.ipynb — Code cells 0-5 (zero-based), especially saved cell 5 output

Waveform Features

Validated · BarkBarkBarkBarkBarkBarkBark/spike_discrim
Revision: a697e68898c688ccc5dfe23ced78dbc6494ca1c1

What was verified
Report checked against metric implementation; source waveform snippets not downloaded or reprocessed.
Measurement definition
KNN-k5 purity is mean same-label neighbor fraction, excluding self. Matched accuracy uses Hungarian cluster-to-label assignment. ARI is chance-corrected clustering agreement.
Test conditions
38,145 spike snippets; 10 putative O-Sort labels; tier-3 features; event-family WeightBank with 10 bins, sigma_scale 1.0, threshold 0.5; KMeans 10 clusters, n_init 10, seed 42.
Result
Event family: KNN-k5 purity 0.9504; matched clustering accuracy 0.5426; ARI 0.3665.
Limitations & applicability
Offline label-agreement analysis, not held-out classification accuracy, biological ground truth, or online sorting validation. Class imbalance and curated-snippet selection limit generalization.
